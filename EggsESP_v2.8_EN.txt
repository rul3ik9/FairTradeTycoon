    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT IS NEW IN v2.8  --  ANTI-AFK
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Roblox disconnects a client after roughly twenty minutes without input. That
    is a problem for every mode in this file, because the script moves your
    character but never touches your mouse or keyboard -- as far as Roblox is
    concerned you have not been at the machine all night.

    Two layers, both switched by "Anti-AFK" in the Automation card:

      1. THE IDLED HOOK. Player.Idled fires when Roblox has decided you are idle,
         which is the moment before it acts on that. The handler sends a right
         mouse click through VirtualUser. A right click is the safest possible
         answer: it resets the idle timer, it does not move the character, it
         does not press anything the game binds, and it cannot land on a button.

      2. A PERIODIC NUDGE, every Config.AntiAFKInterval seconds, as a backstop
         for the case where Idled does not fire at all (it behaves differently
         between executors). The same right click, plus a small jump if
         Config.AntiAFKJump is on.

    THE JUMP IS GATED, and this matters more than it looks. Jumping in the middle
    of a flight fights the movement loop for control of the character, and
    jumping during a pickup breaks the pin that is holding you on the egg -- the
    exact failure v2.4 was written to fix. So it is skipped whenever the script
    is flying you somewhere or holding you in place, and it only ever fires when
    you are standing still doing nothing. That is also when a jump looks most
    like a real player killing time.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT CHANGED IN v2.7
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

    1. MINIMISING MOVED THE WINDOW, AND COULD PUSH IT OFF SCREEN.
       The frame's AnchorPoint was (0.5, 0.5), which means it is positioned by
       its CENTRE. Shrinking it to the title bar therefore pulled the title bar
       inwards towards that centre, and expanding it again pushed it back out --
       so a window parked in a corner walked off the edge, taking the title bar
       (the only thing you can drag) with it.

       v2.7 anchors at (0, 0), the top-left, so a resize keeps the top-left
       exactly where it is and the title bar never moves. On top of that,
       clampWindow() runs after every drag and every resize and pulls the frame
       back inside the viewport, so it cannot be lost even if something else
       moves it.

    2. THE MENU IS NOW SEE-THROUGH.
       Config.UITransparency (0.5) is applied to every panel, card, button and
       row, so the game stays visible behind the menu. Text is untouched and
       stays fully opaque.

       The mechanism is worth knowing: after the interface is built, one pass
       walks the tree and makes every element whose BackgroundTransparency is
       exactly 0 semi-transparent. Anything that must stay solid -- the switch
       knobs -- is created at 0.05 instead of 0, so the pass skips it. A single
       rule, no list of exceptions to maintain.

    3. THE RARE EGG HUNTER NOW FLIES SLOWLY.
       Config.RareApproachSpeed (90 studs/s, against 500 everywhere else). This
       was the wrong way round before: the hunter deliberately waits around for
       fifteen seconds, then covered the distance at thirty times walking speed.
       Flying slowly spends the same wall-clock time doing something that looks
       like travel instead of hovering, so most cycles now reach SafeCycleMin on
       their own and the stationary hold shrinks to a few seconds or vanishes.

    4. GOING HOME RELOCATED YOU SEVERAL TIMES IN A ROW.
       The destination is fine -- dropping into the plot's pivot, under the
       floor, is how the game's own relocation works, and it stays. What was
       wrong is how OFTEN it happened: one hunter cycle issued two trips towards
       the plot within a few seconds (the hold point, then the entry), and any
       loop that ended near home issued another on top. Repeatedly triggering a
       relocation within a couple of seconds is a pattern; doing it once per
       cycle is not.

       v2.7 adds two guards in Section 14: if you are already within
       HomeArriveRadius of home the trip is skipped entirely, and a relocation
       cannot be issued within HomeCooldown seconds of the last one. The
       manual TP Home button bypasses both, because that is you asking.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT IS NEW IN v2.6  --  THE INTERFACE, AND SCREEN ALERTS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    THE MENU WAS REBUILT. Every previous version positioned each widget at a
    hard-coded pixel offset, written out twice -- once for PC and once for
    Mobile. That is why the egg list ran off the bottom of the window: the
    offsets were tuned against one window height and nothing checked that the
    last element still fitted.

    v2.6 replaces all of it with layout objects. Rows are stacked by
    UIListLayout, cards size themselves to their contents, the body is a
    ScrollingFrame with AutomaticCanvasSize, and the window is clamped to the
    camera's viewport when it opens. Content that does not fit now scrolls
    instead of disappearing, and adding a control is one function call with no
    numbers to re-derive. setPCMode and setMobileMode are gone; the device choice
    now only picks a size and a UIScale.

    The look changed with it: dark cards, real switches instead of ON/OFF text
    buttons, a segmented control for the movement mode, and one Theme table
    holding every colour and spacing value.

    SCREEN ALERTS. The Rare Egg Hunter can sit idle for a long time, and a
    status line in the corner of a menu is no use when you are not looking at it.
    So the alert takes the whole screen:

        hunter armed        -> one short purple splash
        Blackhole found     -> black / purple blink, 2.5 seconds
        Cherub found        -> white / yellow blink, 2.5 seconds
        anything else rare  -> Config.DefaultFXColors

    The overlay is a non-interactive Frame in its own high-DisplayOrder
    ScreenGui, so it draws over the game's UI without ever costing you a tap.
    The "Screen alerts" switch in the Automation card turns it off, and colours,
    durations and the blink rate are all in Config.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT IS NEW IN v2.5  --  THE RARE EGG HUNTER
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    A fourth button: "Rare Egg Hunter". It does one job and does it patiently.

        watch the egg folder for a rare spawn (Blackhole / Cherub by default)
        the instant one appears -> noclip-fly to it and collect it
        then fly to a point NEAR your plot and STOP SHORT of it
        hold there until the whole cycle is old enough to be safe
        only then fly the last stretch in

    ALWAYS FLIES, NEVER TELEPORTS. Every other button in this menu obeys the
    AutoFarm / Teleport switch. This one ignores it completely and always uses
    the noclip flight, in both directions, including the final entry onto the
    plot. An instant CFrame jump is the one movement the server can recognise
    with no ambiguity at all, and the whole point of this button is to not be
    recognised. The switch stays where it is for every other feature.

    WHY THE HOLD. Doing the full loop -- leave, grab a rare egg, and be back on
    your plot -- inside about 10-15 seconds is the pattern that gets flagged. No
    legitimate player crosses the map and returns that fast. The hunter therefore
    times the cycle from the moment it leaves, and if the whole trip would land
    inside that window it waits out the difference OUTSIDE the plot, where
    standing still is unremarkable, before covering the last stretch.

    Config.SafeCycleMin (16s) is the floor it waits for, Config.SafeCycleMax
    (19s) is the ceiling it warns you about if a slow server pushed the cycle
    past it. Both are in Section 2 -- tune them if the game's own threshold
    turns out to be different from 10-15s.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT CHANGED IN v2.4  --  WHY FARMING USED TO BE INCONSISTENT
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    v2.3 flew to an egg and then *assumed* the pickup worked. Five separate
    things made that assumption wrong on a regular basis. All five are fixed
    here, and each fix is commented at the line it lives on.

      1. MOMENTUM. Flying was done by writing root.CFrame every frame, which
         builds up a large physics velocity. The moment noclip was switched off
         the character was launched off the egg -- often before the E hold had
         finished. Fix: velocity is zeroed every frame and on arrival.

      2. NOTHING HELD YOU IN PLACE. During the 2-3 second E hold, gravity simply
         pulled the character off the egg, so the prompt went out of range and
         the collect silently failed. Fix: pinRoot() re-writes the CFrame every
         frame for the whole interaction.

      3. NO VERIFICATION. The egg was marked "processed" whether or not it was
         ever collected, so a failed pickup was never retried -- it was skipped
         forever. Fix: collectEgg() waits for the egg to actually despawn and
         retries before giving up.

      4. KEYSTROKE-ONLY INTERACTION. A synthesised E press can be dropped, and
         it depends on the prompt's HoldDuration. Fix: fire the ProximityPrompt
         directly when the executor supports it, with the key hold as fallback.

      5. FARM ORDER BY NAME. v2.3 collected alphabetically, which sent you
         criss-crossing the map; long flights time out far more often. Fix:
         nearest-first ordering.

    Also fixed: stuck-flight detection, stale destination (the egg's position is
    re-read every frame), a permanently-frozen character if a farm loop was
    cancelled mid-interaction, state left stale after dying mid-flight, list
    distances that never updated, a hover animation that drifted, the list
    rebuilding many times per second, and the autoFarmProcessed memory leak.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT THIS SCRIPT IS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    A client-side cheat menu for the Roblox game "Ride A Pet". It builds its own
    GUI inside your PlayerGui and does three things:

        1. ESP        Makes eggs glow through walls, with name + live distance.
        2. MOVEMENT   Puts your character on top of an egg, either by flying
                      (noclip) or by instant teleport.
        3. AUTOMATION Loops "go to egg -> hold E -> go home" by itself, in
                      three flavours: a name-queued farm, a single-target farm,
                      and the rare-egg hunter that paces itself (Section 16B).

    Everything happens on YOUR client. The script does not politely ask the
    server to move you; it writes HumanoidRootPart.CFrame directly and fakes
    key presses with VirtualInputManager.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    HOW TO USE
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Run it -> a "Choose Device" box appears -> pick PC or Mobile.
    The main window then opens with:

        AutoFarm Mode / Teleport Mode   how movement is performed
        ESP All: OFF                    yellow highlight on every egg
        ESP: all eggs                   yellow highlight + live distance
        Auto Best Egg                   endlessly farms Config.BestEggName
        Rare Egg Hunter                 waits for a Blackhole / Cherub egg,
                                        grabs it, then paces the trip home so
                                        the round trip is never suspiciously
                                        fast. Always flies -- this one switch
                                        ignores the movement mode on purpose.
        Screen alerts                   flash the screen when a rare egg is
                                        found, colour-coded per egg type
        Anti-AFK                        keeps Roblox from disconnecting you for
                                        inactivity while the script farms
        Teleport to my plot             go home, obeying the movement mode
        TP Home key: [T]                click, then press any key to rebind
        Show Egg List                   the scrolling list of every egg

    Each list row is:  [icon] [name] [distance] [TP] [AutoFarm] [ESP]

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    THE DATA MODEL  --  understand this and the rest of the file follows
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    The whole program state lives in three tables:

      TABLE               KEYED BY          MEANING
      -----------------   ---------------   -------------------------------------
      eggData             egg Instance      its Highlight + BillboardGui, and
                                            whether its green ESP is toggled on
      autoFarmEggs        egg NAME string   this egg TYPE is queued for farming
      autoFarmProcessed   egg Instance      already collected -> skip this object

    The Instance / Name split is deliberate:
      * Queue by NAME so eggs that respawn later, as brand new objects of the
        same type, keep getting farmed.
      * Skip-list by INSTANCE so the loop does not re-target the exact object it
        just collected while the server is still despawning it.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    PROGRAM FLOW AT A GLANCE
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
      startup
        -> grab services, read Config, find Workspace.RenderedEggs
        -> define ESP system, movement system, automation loops
        -> build the GUI
        -> connect ChildAdded / ChildRemoved so new eggs join the ESP and list
        -> start one background loop that refreshes distance text 5x per second
        -> wait for the user to pick PC or Mobile, then show the window

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WARNING
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Automating gameplay and disabling collisions violates the Roblox Terms of
    Service. Running this can get the account banned. The documentation here is
    for understanding how the code works.

============================================================================ ]]


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 1  --  SERVICES
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Caches every Roblox service the script uses.
    WHY   game:GetService() is a function call. Doing it once at the top and
          storing the result in a local is faster than calling it repeatedly,
          and locals are faster than globals in Lua.
    LEARN What each service is for:
            Players             -> reaching LocalPlayer

            RunService          -> per-frame loop (Heartbeat) for the fly code

            Workspace           -> the 3D world, where eggs and plots live
============================================================================ ]]

--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 5  --  SMALL HELPERS

    WHY   LocalPlayer.Character is nil while you are dead or respawning, and

    LEARN HumanoidRootPart is the invisible part at the centre of a character.

end

--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 6  --  EGG ICON LOOKUP
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Finds the game's own thumbnail image for an egg, by name.
    WHY   Rather than hard-coding asset IDs that break on every game update,
          this reads the game's existing inventory UI and reuses whatever image
          the game itself is already showing. Free, always up to date.
    LEARN The chained "local x = a and a:FindFirstChild(...)" pattern. In Lua,
          "and" short-circuits: if "main" is nil the FindFirstChild is never
          called, so the chain cannot error out at any level. Returning ""
          rather than nil matters because ImageLabel.Image expects a string.

    PATH  PlayerGui > Main > Index > Holders > EggsHolder > <eggName> > ImageLabel
============================================================================ ]]
local function getEggImage(eggName)
    local playerGui = LocalPlayer:FindFirstChild("PlayerGui")
    if not playerGui then
        return ""
    end

    local main       = playerGui:FindFirstChild("Main")
    local index      = main and main:FindFirstChild("Index")
    local holders    = index and index:FindFirstChild("Holders")
    local eggsHolder = holders and holders:FindFirstChild("EggsHolder")
    if not eggsHolder then
        return ""
    end

    local eggFrame = eggsHolder:FindFirstChild(eggName)
    if not eggFrame then
        return ""
    end

    local imageLabel = eggFrame:FindFirstChild("ImageLabel")
    if imageLabel and imageLabel:IsA("ImageLabel") then
        return imageLabel.Image or ""
    end

    return ""
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 7  --  GEOMETRY HELPERS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Position / distance maths that works for BOTH Models and single Parts.
    WHY   An egg in this game might be a Model (several parts grouped) or a bare
          BasePart. Those expose their position differently, so one helper
          normalises them and the other ~15 call sites never have to care.
    LEARN CFrame = position AND rotation in one value.
          Model:GetPivot()  -> the model's CFrame
          BasePart.CFrame   -> the part's CFrame
          Vector3 subtraction gives an offset; .Magnitude turns it into a
          scalar distance in studs.

          Returning math.huge (infinity) on failure instead of nil is a nice
          trick: math.huge still compares correctly with "<", so the sort
          function later cannot crash on a missing egg -- unreachable eggs just
          sink to the bottom of the list.
============================================================================ ]]
local function getTargetCFrame(target)
    if not target or not target.Parent then
        return nil
    end
    if target:IsA("Model") then
        return target:GetPivot()
    end
    if target:IsA("BasePart") then
        return target.CFrame
    end
    return nil
end

local function getTargetPosition(target)
    local targetCFrame = getTargetCFrame(target)
    if not targetCFrame then
        return nil
    end
    return targetCFrame.Position
end

local function getDistanceToTarget(target)
    local root           = getRootPart()
    local targetPosition = getTargetPosition(target)
    if not root or not targetPosition then
        return math.huge
    end
    return (root.Position - targetPosition).Magnitude
end

-- "Still a real egg, still inside the folder." Checking Parent == the folder
-- (rather than just "not nil") also rejects eggs that were reparented away.
-- Defined here in v2.4 rather than down in the AutoFarm section, because the
-- targeting helpers above the GUI now use it too.
local function isValidEgg(egg)
    return egg
        and egg.Parent == RenderedEggsFolder
        and (egg:IsA("Model") or egg:IsA("BasePart"))
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 8  --  TWEEN HELPER
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  One-liner that smoothly animates any GUI property.
    WHY   Raw TweenService is three lines every time (build TweenInfo, Create,
          Play). This wraps it, supplies a house style (Quart easing, Out
          direction), and guards against animating an object that was already
          destroyed.
    LEARN tween(button, {BackgroundTransparency = 0.1}, 0.2)
          -> over 0.2 seconds, glide BackgroundTransparency to 0.1.
          Easing "Out" means it starts fast and settles gently, which is what
          makes UI feel responsive rather than floaty.
============================================================================ ]]
local function tween(object, properties, duration)
    if not object or not object.Parent then
        return
    end
    local info = TweenInfo.new(
        duration or Config.AnimationTime,
        Enum.EasingStyle.Quart,
        Enum.EasingDirection.Out
    )
    TweenService:Create(object, info, properties):Play()
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 9  --  ESP, PART 1: THE FLOATING LABEL
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Builds the "name + distance" text that hovers over an egg.
    WHY   A BillboardGui is a 2D GUI attached to a 3D object that always turns
          to face the camera. Two properties do the heavy lifting:
            AlwaysOnTop = true  -> draws THROUGH walls (this is the "wallhack")
            MaxDistance = 2000  -> stops rendering past 2000 studs, for framerate
          It is created Enabled = false and switched on later by updateEggESP,
          because creating instances is expensive and toggling .Enabled is not.
    LEARN StudsOffset = Vector3.new(0, 3.5, 0) lifts the label 3.5 studs above
          the egg so it does not overlap the model.
============================================================================ ]]
local function createEggLabel(egg)
    local data = eggData[egg]
    if not data then
        return
    end
    -- Already built and still alive? Nothing to do. This guard is what makes the
    -- function safe to call every single time the ESP refreshes.
    if data.NameBillboard and data.NameBillboard.Parent then
        return
    end

    local billboard = Instance.new("BillboardGui")
    billboard.Name        = "EggESP_Info"
    billboard.Size        = UDim2.new(0, 180, 0, 45)
    billboard.StudsOffset = Vector3.new(0, 3.5, 0)  -- float above the egg
    billboard.AlwaysOnTop = true                    -- draw through walls
    billboard.MaxDistance = 2000
    billboard.Enabled     = false                   -- switched on by updateEggESP
    billboard.Parent      = egg

    local nameLabel = Instance.new("TextLabel")
    nameLabel.Name                   = "EggName"
    nameLabel.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, 23)
    nameLabel.BackgroundTransparency = 1     -- text only, no box behind it
    nameLabel.Text                   = egg.Name
    nameLabel.TextColor3             = Color3.fromRGB(255, 255, 255)
    nameLabel.TextStrokeTransparency = 0.35  -- dark outline keeps it readable
                                             -- against a bright sky
    nameLabel.TextSize               = Config.ESPNameSize
    nameLabel.Font                   = Enum.Font.SourceSansBold
    nameLabel.Parent                 = billboard

    local distanceLabel = Instance.new("TextLabel")
    distanceLabel.Name                   = "Distance"
    distanceLabel.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, 18)
    distanceLabel.Position               = UDim2.new(0, 0, 0, 22)  -- under the name
    distanceLabel.BackgroundTransparency = 1
    distanceLabel.Text                   = "0 studs"
    distanceLabel.TextColor3             = Color3.fromRGB(210, 210, 210)
    distanceLabel.TextStrokeTransparency = 0.4
    distanceLabel.TextSize               = Config.ESPDistanceSize
    distanceLabel.Font                   = Enum.Font.SourceSans
    distanceLabel.Parent                 = billboard

    data.NameBillboard = billboard
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ESP, PART 2: REFRESHING ONE LABEL'S TEXT
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Rewrites the name and distance strings of an existing billboard.
    WHY   Distance changes as you move, so this is called about 5 times per
          second by the background loop at the bottom of the file. It does the
          minimum possible work: no instance creation, just two string writes.
    LEARN math.floor(d + 0.5) is rounding to the nearest integer. string.format
          with "%d" then prints it without a decimal point.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function updateEggLabel(egg)
    local data = eggData[egg]
    if not data or not data.NameBillboard then
        return
    end

    local billboard = data.NameBillboard
    if not billboard.Parent then
        return
    end

    local nameLabel     = billboard:FindFirstChild("EggName")
    local distanceLabel = billboard:FindFirstChild("Distance")

    if nameLabel then
        nameLabel.Text = egg.Name
    end

    if distanceLabel then
        local distance = getDistanceToTarget(egg)
        if distance == math.huge then
            distanceLabel.Text = "?"   -- character is dead / egg unreachable
        else
            distanceLabel.Text = string.format("%d studs", math.floor(distance + 0.5))
        end
    end
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 10  --  ESP, PART 3: THE DECISION FUNCTION
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  The heart of the ESP system. For ONE egg it answers: should this glow,
          and in what colour? Then it creates / enables / disables the visuals.
    WHY   Every other ESP function funnels into this one, so the priority rule
          lives in exactly one place:

              green per-egg ESP   BEATS
              yellow global ESP   BEATS
              off

    LEARN Two patterns worth stealing:

          1. LAZY INITIALISATION. If eggData[egg] does not exist yet, it is
             created on the spot. That means no separate "register this egg"
             step is ever needed -- just call updateEggESP on anything.

          2. CREATE ONCE, TOGGLE FOREVER. The Highlight is never destroyed when
             the ESP is turned off; only .Enabled flips to false. Instance
             creation is expensive; setting a boolean is nearly free. Turning
             ESP on and off repeatedly therefore costs almost nothing.

          Highlight is a modern Roblox object that outlines and tints a model
          even through geometry. Before it existed, people faked this with
          SelectionBox or BoxHandleAdornment.
============================================================================ ]]
local function updateEggESP(egg)
    if not egg then
        return
    end
    if not egg:IsA("Model") and not egg:IsA("BasePart") then
        return
    end

    -- Lazily create the record for this egg (see LEARN note 1 above).
    if not eggData[egg] then
        eggData[egg] = {
            Highlight     = nil,
            NameBillboard = nil,
            CustomColor   = Config.CustomESPColor,
            CustomActive  = false
        }
    end

    local data       = eggData[egg]
    local shouldShow = false
    local color      = Config.GlobalESPColor

    -- The priority rule. Individual ESP wins over the global one.
    if data.CustomActive then
        shouldShow = true
        color      = data.CustomColor or Config.CustomESPColor
    elseif mainESPActive then
        shouldShow = true
        color      = Config.GlobalESPColor
    end

    if shouldShow then
        -- Build the Highlight only once per egg (or if something destroyed it).
        if not data.Highlight or not data.Highlight.Parent then
            local highlight = Instance.new("Highlight")
            highlight.Name                = "EggESP_Highlight"
            highlight.Adornee             = egg  -- which object to outline
            highlight.FillTransparency    = Config.ESPFillTransparency
            highlight.OutlineTransparency = Config.ESPOutlineTransparency
            highlight.Parent              = egg
            data.Highlight = highlight
        end

        data.Highlight.FillColor    = color
        data.Highlight.OutlineColor = color
        data.Highlight.Enabled      = true

        createEggLabel(egg)
        if data.NameBillboard then
            data.NameBillboard.Enabled = true
        end
        updateEggLabel(egg)
    else
        -- See LEARN note 2: disable, never destroy.
        if data.Highlight then
            data.Highlight.Enabled = false
        end
        if data.NameBillboard then
            data.NameBillboard.Enabled = false
        end
    end
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ESP, PART 4: BULK OPERATIONS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Apply the decision function to every egg at once.
    WHY   applyGlobalESP is what the "ESP All" button calls: flip the flag, then
          let updateEggESP re-derive the correct visual state for each egg. The
          button never touches Highlights directly -- one source of truth.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function updateAllESP()
    if not RenderedEggsFolder then
        return
    end
    for _, egg in ipairs(RenderedEggsFolder:GetChildren()) do
        updateEggESP(egg)
    end
end

local function applyGlobalESP(state)
    mainESPActive = state
    updateAllESP()
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ESP, PART 5: CLEAN UP A DESPAWNED EGG
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Destroys an egg's visuals and forgets it.
    WHY   Called from the ChildRemoved event. Setting eggData[egg] = nil removes
          the last reference the script holds, which lets Lua's garbage collector
          reclaim the memory. Without this the table would grow forever on a
          busy server.
    v2.4: also clears autoFarmProcessed[egg] and listDistanceLabels[egg]. v2.3
          only cleared eggData, so those two tables grew for the whole session
          -- one entry per egg that ever spawned.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function removeEggData(egg)
    local data = eggData[egg]
    if not data then
        return
    end
    if data.Highlight then
        data.Highlight:Destroy()
    end
    if data.NameBillboard then
        data.NameBillboard:Destroy()
    end
    eggData[egg] = nil
    -- v2.3 left these two behind, so both tables grew for the whole session.
    autoFarmProcessed[egg]   = nil
    listDistanceLabels[egg]  = nil
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 11  --  MOVEMENT, PART 1: INSTANT TELEPORT
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Snaps the character on top of a target in a single frame.
    WHY   This is the classic Roblox teleport, and it is one line of real work:

              root.CFrame = targetCFrame * CFrame.new(0, TPHeight, 0)

    LEARN Multiplying CFrames COMPOSES them. "targetCFrame * CFrame.new(0,3,0)"
          means "the target's position and rotation, then move 3 studs up along
          the target's own up-axis". The +3 offset stops you spawning inside the
          egg's collision box and being shoved somewhere random by physics.

          The reason this works at all is that Roblox lets a client own the
          physics of its own character, so the server accepts the new position.
          That single design fact is what all Roblox movement exploits rest on.
============================================================================ ]]
--[[ v2.4 ADDITION -- THE TWO FUNCTIONS THAT FIX MOST OF THE INCONSISTENCY

     zeroVelocity  Writing root.CFrame every frame is not "moving", it is
                   teleporting 8 studs at a time. Roblox's physics solver sees
                   those jumps and hands the assembly a matching velocity. By
                   the time the flight ends the character is carrying hundreds
                   of studs/second of momentum, so the instant collisions come
                   back it is flung off the egg. Writing the velocity back to
                   zero every frame keeps the character exactly where we put it.

     pinRoot       Arriving is not enough: the game wants you standing there for
                   2-3 seconds while the prompt fills. Gravity does not care.
                   pinRoot re-writes the CFrame every Heartbeat until you call
                   the returned stop function, so the interaction cannot drift
                   out of range.

                   The pinToken generation counter is the safety mechanism. Each
                   call claims the next token; the loop exits as soon as the
                   token changes. stopMovement bumps it, so even if the farming
                   coroutine is cancelled mid-pin (task.cancel never runs your
                   cleanup code) the character is released. PinMaxTime is a
                   second, independent backstop.
]]
local function zeroVelocity(root)
    if not root then return end
    -- pcall: these properties do not exist on very old Roblox builds, and some
    -- executors sandbox them. Failing to zero velocity should never be fatal.
    pcall(function()
        root.AssemblyLinearVelocity  = Vector3.zero
        root.AssemblyAngularVelocity = Vector3.zero
    end)
end

-- maxTime is optional and defaults to Config.PinMaxTime. The Rare Egg Hunter
-- passes a longer one, because it deliberately holds position for well over ten
-- seconds while it waits out the safety window.
local function pinRoot(cframe, maxTime)
    pinToken += 1
    local myToken = pinToken

    task.spawn(function()
        local deadline = os.clock() + (maxTime or Config.PinMaxTime)
        pinOwner = myToken
        while pinToken == myToken and os.clock() < deadline do
            local root = getRootPart()
            if not root then break end
            root.CFrame = cframe
            zeroVelocity(root)
            RunService.Heartbeat:Wait()
        end
        -- Clear the flag only if this loop is still the owner, so a newer pin
        -- taking over is not switched off by the old one finishing.
        --
        -- Note this compares pinOwner, NOT pinToken. pinToken is what gets
        -- bumped to END this loop -- stopMovement does exactly that -- so
        -- testing it here would be false on every cancelled pin and leave the
        -- flag stuck on forever, silently disabling every later anti-AFK hop.
        -- Two counters because they answer two different questions: "should I
        -- stop?" and "am I the one holding this?".
        if pinOwner == myToken then
            pinOwner = 0
        end
    end)

    return function()
        if pinToken == myToken then
            pinToken += 1
        end
    end
end

--[[ The flight marker. moveToModel takes an Instance, but plenty of useful
     destinations are bare coordinates -- "45 studs short of the plot", "three
     studs above the plot's floor". Rather than duplicate the flight loop for raw
     Vector3s, this drops a transparent anchored Part there, flies to it and
     destroys it. Parts an exploit creates on the client are never replicated to
     the server, so nobody else ever sees it. ]]
local function makeFlightMarker(position)
    local marker = Instance.new("Part")
    marker.Name         = "EggsESP_FlightMarker"
    marker.Size         = Vector3.new(1, 1, 1)
    marker.Anchored     = true
    marker.CanCollide   = false
    marker.Transparency = 1
    -- pcall: CanQuery and CanTouch do not exist on older Roblox builds, and a
    -- missing property here should not take anything down.
    pcall(function()
        marker.CanQuery = false
        marker.CanTouch = false
    end)
    marker.Parent = Workspace
    return marker
end

local function teleportToModel(target)
    local root = getRootPart()
    if not root then return false end

    local targetCFrame = getTargetCFrame(target)
    if not targetCFrame then return false end

    root.CFrame = targetCFrame * CFrame.new(0, Config.TPHeight, 0)
    zeroVelocity(root)  -- v2.4: a teleport inherits the velocity you had before
                        -- it, which used to slide you straight back off the egg.
    return true
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 12  --  MOVEMENT, PART 2: NOCLIP
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Turns collisions off for the whole character, and back on again.
    WHY   Flying in a straight line to an egg means passing through walls, trees
          and terrain. Without noclip the character would jam against the first
          obstacle and the loop would sit there until the timeout.
    LEARN The important idea here is the SNAPSHOT.

          A naive restore does "part.CanCollide = true" for everything. That is
          a bug: hats, accessories and some decorative parts are SUPPOSED to be
          non-collidable, and blindly setting them true breaks the character.

          This version stores each part's original value in movementPartsState
          before changing it, then writes those exact values back. The
          "if part and part.Parent" check handles the case where the character
          respawned mid-flight and the old parts no longer exist.
============================================================================ ]]
local function setNoclip(enabled)
    local character = getCharacter()
    if not character then return end

    if enabled then
        movementPartsState = {}
        for _, part in ipairs(character:GetDescendants()) do
            if part:IsA("BasePart") then
                movementPartsState[part] = part.CanCollide  -- remember
                part.CanCollide = false                     -- then disable
            end
        end
    elseif movementPartsState then
        for part, oldCanCollide in pairs(movementPartsState) do
            if part and part.Parent then part.CanCollide = oldCanCollide end
        end
        movementPartsState = nil
    end
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 13  --  MOVEMENT, PART 3: THE FLY LOOP
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Moves the character to a target, using whichever mode is selected.
          Returns true ONLY if it actually arrived.
    WHY   A teleport is instant and obvious. Flying at a fixed speed looks more
          like a (very fast) player and gives the server a continuous stream of
          plausible positions instead of one impossible jump.

    THE CORE MATHS, line by line:

          offset   = destination - root.Position   -- vector pointing at the goal
          distance = offset.Magnitude              -- how far, in studs
          dt       = Heartbeat:Wait()              -- seconds since last frame
          step     = min(distance, Speed * dt)     -- how far to move THIS frame
          root.CFrame = root.CFrame + offset.Unit * step

          * offset.Unit is the direction with length 1, so multiplying it by
            "step" gives a movement of exactly "step" studs in that direction.
          * Multiplying by dt makes the speed frame-rate independent: at 30 FPS
            each step is twice as big as at 60 FPS, and you arrive at the same
            time either way.
          * math.min(distance, ...) clamps the final step so you never overshoot
            and oscillate around the target.
          * "root.CFrame + vector" moves the position but KEEPS the rotation,
            which is why AutoRotate is disabled -- otherwise the humanoid would
            keep trying to spin the character back to face its walk direction.

    THE FOUR EXIT CONDITIONS -- each one prevents a specific hang:

          1. movementActive went false      -> user pressed Stop
          2. os.clock() exceeded maxTime    -> path blocked, give up
          3. target.Parent is nil           -> someone else collected the egg
          4. getRootPart() ~= root          -> we died and respawned mid-flight

    LEARN "movementActive" doubles as a lock. The early
          "if movementActive then return false end" guarantees only one flight
          can run at a time, so two automation loops cannot fight over the
          character's position.
============================================================================ ]]
-- forceFly (v2.5) makes this function ignore the AutoFarm / Teleport switch and
-- always take the noclip flight path. The Rare Egg Hunter passes it on every
-- single move it makes: an instant CFrame jump is the least deniable thing a
-- client can do, so the one mode built around not being noticed never uses it.
local function moveToModel(target, forceFly, speed)
    -- v2.4: the lock now covers Teleport mode too. v2.3 returned before taking
    -- it, so a manual teleport could fire in the middle of an AutoFarm flight
    -- and both would fight over root.CFrame.
    if movementActive then return false end

    local root         = getRootPart()
    local targetCFrame = getTargetCFrame(target)
    if not root or not targetCFrame then return false end

    if movementMode == "Teleport" and not forceFly then
        movementActive = true
        local ok = teleportToModel(target)
        movementActive = false
        return ok
    end

    local character = getCharacter()
    local humanoid  = character and character:FindFirstChildOfClass("Humanoid")
    if not humanoid then return false end

    -- v2.7: the speed is per-call. The Rare Egg Hunter passes a much slower one;
    -- everything else gets Config.MovementSpeed. Note maxTime is derived from
    -- the SAME value -- deriving it from the global constant would give a slow
    -- flight a fast flight's time budget and abort it halfway.
    local moveSpeed = speed or Config.MovementSpeed

    movementActive      = true
    movementHumanoid    = humanoid
    local oldAutoRotate = humanoid.AutoRotate
    movementAutoRotate  = oldAutoRotate
    local success       = false
    local startTime     = os.clock()
    local startDistance = (root.Position - targetCFrame.Position).Magnitude
    -- Expected travel time, plus 2s slack, with a 3s floor.
    local maxTime       = math.max(3, (startDistance / moveSpeed) + 2)

    -- Stuck detection. bestDistance is the closest we have ever been; if it does
    -- not improve for StuckTimeout seconds we are wedged on something and the
    -- remaining budget is wasted time. v2.3 always burned the full maxTime.
    local bestDistance = math.huge
    local lastProgress = os.clock()

    setNoclip(true)
    humanoid.AutoRotate = false

    while movementActive and os.clock() - startTime <= maxTime do
        if not target or not target.Parent then break end  -- egg got collected
        if getRootPart() ~= root then break end            -- we respawned

        -- v2.4: the destination is re-read EVERY frame. v2.3 snapshotted it once
        -- before take-off, so any egg that moved, re-pivoted or finished
        -- streaming in after the flight started was flown past, not to.
        local liveCFrame = getTargetCFrame(target)
        if not liveCFrame then break end

        local destination = liveCFrame.Position + Vector3.new(0, Config.TPHeight, 0)
        local offset      = destination - root.Position
        local distance    = offset.Magnitude

        if distance <= Config.ArrivalRadius then
            root.CFrame = liveCFrame * CFrame.new(0, Config.TPHeight, 0)
            zeroVelocity(root)
            success = true
            break
        end

        if distance < bestDistance - 0.5 then
            bestDistance = distance
            lastProgress = os.clock()
        elseif os.clock() - lastProgress > Config.StuckTimeout then
            break
        end

        -- Frame-rate independent step, clamped so we never overshoot.
        local dt   = RunService.Heartbeat:Wait()
        local step = math.min(distance, moveSpeed * dt)
        root.CFrame = root.CFrame + offset.Unit * step
        zeroVelocity(root)  -- the single most important line in this loop
    end

    -- Always restore, whichever exit we took.
    movementActive = false
    if humanoid.Parent then humanoid.AutoRotate = oldAutoRotate end
    movementHumanoid   = nil
    movementAutoRotate = nil
    setNoclip(false)
    zeroVelocity(getRootPart())  -- drop whatever momentum the last frame added
    return success
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MOVEMENT, PART 4: ABORT
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Cancels any flight in progress and puts the character back to normal.
    WHY   Setting movementActive = false makes the while-loop above exit on its
          next iteration. This function then force-restores AutoRotate and
          collisions itself, because the automation loops call task.cancel()
          immediately afterwards -- and a cancelled coroutine never reaches its
          own cleanup code. Order matters: ALWAYS stopMovement() BEFORE
          task.cancel(), or you can be left permanently noclipped.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function stopMovement()
    movementActive = false
    pinToken += 1  -- v2.4: releases any pinRoot loop. Without this, stopping a
                   -- farm during an interaction left the character frozen.
    if movementHumanoid and movementHumanoid.Parent then
        -- v2.4: restore the value we actually saw, not a hard-coded true.
        movementHumanoid.AutoRotate = (movementAutoRotate == nil) or movementAutoRotate
    end
    movementHumanoid   = nil
    movementAutoRotate = nil
    setNoclip(false)
    zeroVelocity(getRootPart())
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MOVEMENT, PART 5: THE MODE SWITCH
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Repaints the two mode buttons and changes movementMode.
    WHY   updateMovementModeButtons is separated from setMovementMode so it can
          also be called once at GUI build time, to colour the default state
          without triggering any side effects.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function updateMovementModeButtons()
    if not ModeAutoFarmBtn or not ModeTeleportBtn then return end
    if movementMode == "AutoFarm" then

        ModeAutoFarmBtn.TextColor3       = Color3.fromRGB(255, 255, 255)

        ModeTeleportBtn.TextColor3       = Color3.fromRGB(220, 220, 220)
    else
        ModeAutoFarmBtn.BackgroundColor3 = Color3.fromRGB(35, 35, 35)
        ModeAutoFarmBtn.TextColor3       = Color3.fromRGB(220, 220, 220)
        ModeTeleportBtn.BackgroundColor3 = Color3.fromRGB(0, 120, 170)  -- blue = active
        ModeTeleportBtn.TextColor3       = Color3.fromRGB(255, 255, 255)
    end
end

local function setMovementMode(mode)
    if mode ~= "AutoFarm" and mode ~= "Teleport" then return end
    stopMovement()  -- never leave a flight running under the old mode
    movementMode = mode
    updateMovementModeButtons()
    if StatusLabel then
        StatusLabel.Text = mode == "AutoFarm"

    end

--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 14  --  TELEPORT TO YOUR OWN PLOT
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Finds which plot in Workspace.Plots belongs to you, and goes there.
    WHY   The farming loops return home after every egg, presumably because the
          game requires you to be on your plot to hatch or store what you picked
          up.
    LEARN The three-branch owner check is defensive programming worth copying.
          Game developers store "who owns this" inconsistently:
              StringValue  -> compare against LocalPlayer.Name
              ObjectValue  -> compare against the LocalPlayer object itself
              anything else-> fall back to tostring(value) == Name
          Handling all three means the script survives the game changing its
          internal representation.

          Note it calls moveToModel, not teleportToModel, so going home obeys
          the selected movement mode.
============================================================================ ]]
-- Cached between calls: the farm loops go home after every single egg, and
-- re-scanning every plot's Data folder each time is pure waste.
local cachedHomePlot = nil

-- v2.5 splits FINDING your plot from GOING to it. The Rare Egg Hunter needs the
-- plot object itself, so it can work out where "just outside" is without
-- travelling there yet.
local function getHomePlot()
    if cachedHomePlot and cachedHomePlot.Parent then
        return cachedHomePlot
    end
    cachedHomePlot = nil

    local plotsFolder = Workspace:FindFirstChild("Plots")
    if not plotsFolder then
        return nil
    end

    for _, plot in ipairs(plotsFolder:GetChildren()) do
        local dataFolder = plot:FindFirstChild("Data")
        if dataFolder then
            local ownerValue = dataFolder:FindFirstChild("Owner")
            if ownerValue then
                local isOwner = false

                if ownerValue:IsA("StringValue") then
                    isOwner = ownerValue.Value == LocalPlayer.Name
                elseif ownerValue:IsA("ObjectValue") then
                    isOwner = ownerValue.Value == LocalPlayer
                else
                    isOwner = tostring(ownerValue.Value) == LocalPlayer.Name
                end

                if isOwner then
                    cachedHomePlot = plot
                    return plot
                end
            end
        end
    end

    return nil
end

--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    GOING HOME   (reworked in v2.7)
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHERE it goes has not changed. moveToModel(plot) aims at the model's pivot,
    which for a plot sits under the floor -- and dropping into it is how the
    game's own relocation works, so that stays exactly as it was.

    HOW OFTEN it goes is the part that changed. A single Rare Egg Hunter cycle
    used to issue two trips towards the plot within a few seconds of each other
    (out to the hold point, then in), and the other farm loops issued one after
    every egg regardless of whether they had just been home. Triggering a
    relocation three times inside five seconds is a pattern; doing it once per
    cycle is ordinary play. Two guards, both cheap:

      1. ALREADY THERE. Within HomeArriveRadius of home, return true without
         moving. A move you have already completed should not be re-issued.
      2. COOLDOWN. A relocation cannot follow the previous one inside
         HomeCooldown seconds. Reporting success rather than failure is
         deliberate -- the caller's next step is "now go find another egg", and
         that is exactly what it should do.

    Both guards are skipped when `force` is true, which is what the manual
    TP Home button and the hunter's one deliberate entry pass. An explicit press
    is not a pattern, and the hunter's entry comes after a sixteen second wait.

    Config.LandOnPlotSurface flips the destination to a spawn pad or the top of
    the plot instead. It is off by default; it exists for the case where you want
    to arrive standing on the plot rather than being pushed up out of it.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local PLOT_PAD_NAMES = { "Spawn", "SpawnLocation", "SpawnPad", "Pad", "Platform", "Base", "Floor" }
local lastHomeTime   = 0

local function getPlotLandingCFrame(plot)
    if not plot then
        return nil
    end

    if Config.LandOnPlotSurface then
        -- An explicit pad, if the game provides one.
        for _, padName in ipairs(PLOT_PAD_NAMES) do
            local part = plot:FindFirstChild(padName, true)
            if part and part:IsA("BasePart") then
                return part.CFrame * CFrame.new(0, (part.Size.Y / 2) + Config.TPHeight, 0)
            end
        end

        -- Otherwise the top face of the bounding box. pcall because
        -- GetBoundingBox throws on an empty model.
        if plot:IsA("Model") then
            local ok, boxCFrame, boxSize = pcall(function()
                return plot:GetBoundingBox()
            end)
            if ok and boxCFrame and boxSize then
                return CFrame.new(boxCFrame.Position
                    + Vector3.new(0, (boxSize.Y / 2) + Config.TPHeight, 0))
            end
        end
    end

    -- The default, and what every earlier version did: the pivot.
    local pivot = getTargetCFrame(plot)
    return pivot and (pivot * CFrame.new(0, Config.TPHeight, 0)) or nil
end

local function teleportToHomePlot(forceFly, speed, force)
    if not Config.ReturnHome then
        return true
    end

    local plot = getHomePlot()
    if not plot then
        return false
    end

    local landing = getPlotLandingCFrame(plot)
    if not landing then
        return false
    end

    -- Guard 1: already home.
    local root = getRootPart()
    if root and (root.Position - landing.Position).Magnitude <= Config.HomeArriveRadius then
        lastHomeTime = os.clock()
        return true
    end

    -- Guard 2: too soon after the last one.
    if not force and (os.clock() - lastHomeTime) < Config.HomeCooldown then
        return true
    end

    local marker  = makeFlightMarker(landing.Position)
    local arrived = moveToModel(marker, forceFly, speed)
    marker:Destroy()

    if arrived then
        lastHomeTime = os.clock()
    end
    return arrived
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 15  --  AUTOMATION, PART 1: FAKING THE INTERACT KEY
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Presses and holds E for a given number of seconds.
    WHY   The game collects an egg when you hold E near it. Rather than calling
          the game's internal RemoteEvent (which would require reverse
          engineering it, and is easier for the server to detect as malformed),
          the script simply synthesises the same input a real player produces.
    LEARN VirtualInputManager:SendKeyEvent(isPressed, keyCode, isRepeated, target)
          It is NOT available to ordinary game scripts -- only to exploit
          executors. That single call is what separates this from a script you
          could legitimately publish inside a game.

          The press and the release are two separate calls with a wait between,
          which is exactly what "holding" a key means.
============================================================================ ]]
-- Releasing E is its own function because several cleanup paths need it: a
-- cancelled coroutine never reaches its own release, and a key left logically
-- held down breaks every later interaction in the session.
local function releaseEKey()
    VirtualInputManager:SendKeyEvent(false, Enum.KeyCode.E, false, game)
end

local function holdEKey(duration)
    VirtualInputManager:SendKeyEvent(true, Enum.KeyCode.E, false, game)
    task.wait(duration)
    releaseEKey()
end

--[[ v2.4 ADDITION -- INTERACTING RELIABLY

     Roblox collects things through a ProximityPrompt: an object with a
     HoldDuration, a MaxActivationDistance and a Triggered event the server
     listens to. v2.3 only ever synthesised the E key, which means it depended
     on three things it could not control -- the prompt being in range for the
     full hold, the key event not being swallowed by another UI, and the hold
     time in Config matching the prompt's real HoldDuration.

     fireproximityprompt(prompt) is an executor global that raises Triggered
     directly, skipping all three problems. It does not exist in every executor,
     so this falls back to the key hold, and retries always use the key path in
     case the game ignores externally fired prompts.

     collectEgg keeps the character pinned in place around this call, for the
     settle wait, the hold itself and the confirmation window.
]]
local function findPrompt(target)
    if not target then return nil end
    for _, descendant in ipairs(target:GetDescendants()) do
        if descendant:IsA("ProximityPrompt") and descendant.Enabled then
            return descendant
        end
    end
    return nil
end

-- Assumes the caller already has the character pinned in place; see collectEgg.
local function interactWith(target, duration, forceKey)
    local prompt = (not forceKey) and Config.UseProximityPrompt and findPrompt(target) or nil

    if prompt and type(fireproximityprompt) == "function" then
        -- pcall because executor globals vary in signature and a failure here
        -- must fall through to the key path, not kill the farming loop.
        local ok = pcall(fireproximityprompt, prompt)
        task.wait(0.15)
        if not ok then
            holdEKey(duration)
        end
    else
        holdEKey(duration)
    end
end

--[[ v2.4 ADDITION -- THE COLLECT CYCLE, WITH PROOF

     This is the function that makes farming consistent. v2.3's inline version
     was "fly there, hold E, mark it done" -- it never checked whether anything
     happened, so one failed pickup meant that egg was skipped permanently.

     Here, an attempt only counts when the egg actually disappears from the
     world. Up to 1 + InteractRetries attempts are made, and `isActive` is a
     function (not a captured boolean) so the loop re-reads the live stop flag
     after every yield.
]]
local function collectEgg(egg, holdTime, isActive, forceFly, speed)
    for attempt = 1, 1 + Config.InteractRetries do
        if not isActive() then return false end
        if not egg or not egg.Parent then return true end  -- someone else took it

        if moveToModel(egg, forceFly, speed) then
            -- Pin FIRST, before anything that yields. We are hovering
            -- Config.TPHeight studs above the egg with nothing underneath, so
            -- even the short settle wait below is long enough for gravity to
            -- drop the character several studs and take the prompt out of range.
            local root   = getRootPart()
            local anchor = root and root.CFrame
            local unpin  = anchor and pinRoot(anchor) or nil

            task.wait(0.15)  -- one beat for the prompt to notice we are in range

            if not isActive() then
                if unpin then unpin() end
                return false
            end

            if egg.Parent then
                interactWith(egg, holdTime, attempt > 1)
            end

            -- Proof of collection: the server despawns the egg. Poll until it
            -- does, or until we are sure this attempt failed. Stay pinned for
            -- this part too -- the server is still processing the pickup.
            local deadline = os.clock() + Config.CollectConfirmTime
            while os.clock() < deadline do
                if not egg.Parent then
                    if unpin then unpin() end
                    return true
                end
                if not isActive() then
                    if unpin then unpin() end
                    return false
                end
                task.wait(0.1)
            end

            if unpin then unpin() end
        end
    end
    return false
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    AUTOMATION, PART 2: FINDING THE "BEST" EGG
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Returns the NEAREST egg whose name CONTAINS Config.BestEggName.
          (v2.3 returned the first one in folder order, which could be on the
          far side of the map while an identical egg sat ten studs away.)
    LEARN string.find(haystack, needle, 1, true)
          The 4th argument "true" means PLAIN find -- Lua pattern matching is
          disabled. That matters: without it, characters like "-", "(", "%" or
          "." inside an egg name would be interpreted as pattern syntax and
          either match the wrong thing or throw an error. Both :lower() calls
          make the comparison case-insensitive.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function findBestEgg()
    if not RenderedEggsFolder then
        return nil
    end

    -- v2.4: returns the NEAREST match, not whichever one happens to sit first in
    -- the folder. v2.3 could pick an egg on the far side of the map while an
    -- identical one was ten studs away, which is both slower and far more likely
    -- to hit the flight timeout.
    local needle       = Config.BestEggName:lower()
    local best         = nil
    local bestDistance = math.huge

    for _, egg in ipairs(RenderedEggsFolder:GetChildren()) do
        if isValidEgg(egg) and string.find(egg.Name:lower(), needle, 1, true) then
            local distance = getDistanceToTarget(egg)
            if distance < bestDistance then
                best         = egg
                bestDistance = distance
            end
        end
    end

    return best
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    AUTOMATION, PART 3: THE "AUTO BEST EGG" LOOP
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Endlessly hunts one specific egg type.

          while running:
              find an egg matching "cherub"
              found?  -> fly to it -> hold E for 3s -> fly home -> wait 0.8s
              none?   -> wait 0.5s and look again

    WHY   task.spawn runs the function on a NEW coroutine, so the infinite loop
          does not freeze the game or the GUI. The returned handle is saved in
          autoBestEggThread so task.cancel can kill it later.

    LEARN Two habits here are worth copying:

          1. startAutoBestEgg() begins by calling stopAutoBestEgg(). That makes
             it impossible to accidentally run two loops at once, no matter how
             fast the user spams the button.

          2. "if autoBestEggActive" is re-checked after EVERY task.wait. A wait
             yields, and the user may have hit Stop during that yield, so the
             flag must be re-read rather than trusted from before the pause.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function stopAutoBestEgg()
    autoBestEggActive = false
    setAutoBestEggButton(false)
    stopMovement()                      -- restore collisions BEFORE cancelling
    if autoBestEggThread then
        task.cancel(autoBestEggThread)
        autoBestEggThread = nil
    end
    releaseEKey()  -- v2.4: a cancelled coroutine never runs its own key release
end

-- Passed to collectEgg so it can re-read the live flag after every yield.
local function isAutoBestEggActive()
    return autoBestEggActive
end

local function startAutoBestEgg()
    stopAutoBestEgg()  -- never run two loops at once
    -- v2.5: and never run two DIFFERENT loops at once either. Two automation
    -- threads both writing root.CFrame produce a character that visibly stutters
    -- between two destinations and collects nothing.
    if stopAutoFarm then stopAutoFarm() end
    if stopAutoRare then stopAutoRare() end
    autoBestEggActive = true
    setAutoBestEggButton(true)

    autoBestEggThread = task.spawn(function()
        while autoBestEggActive do
            local egg = findBestEgg()

            if egg and egg.Parent then
                setStatus("● Auto Best Egg: " .. egg.Name)

                -- v2.4: the whole fly / interact / verify / retry cycle now
                -- lives in collectEgg, so this loop and AutoFarm below behave
                -- identically instead of each having their own timing bugs.
                local collected = collectEgg(egg, Config.AutoEggHoldTime, isAutoBestEggActive)

                if autoBestEggActive then
                    teleportToHomePlot()           -- go home to hatch/store
                end

                if not collected and autoBestEggActive then
                    setStatus("● Auto Best Egg: pickup not confirmed, retrying",
                        Color3.fromRGB(230, 170, 60))
                end

                task.wait(Config.AutoEggDelay)
            else
                -- Egg not spawned right now: wait and look again.
                task.wait(0.5)
            end
        end
    end)
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 16  --  AUTOMATION, PART 4: THE QUEUE-DRIVEN AUTOFARM
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Farms every egg TYPE the user ticked in the list, then stops.

          while running:
              eggs = spawned eggs whose NAME is queued AND not yet processed,
                     sorted nearest first
              none queued at all?         -> stop and hide the red button
              none spawned right now?     -> wait for a respawn, up to
                                             MaxIdlePasses * IdlePassDelay
              otherwise, take eggs[1] (the closest):
                  fly to it -> pin -> interact -> WAIT FOR IT TO DESPAWN
                            -> retry if it did not -> fly home -> mark processed
              then loop, and re-measure which egg is closest from here

    WHY   This is the "queue by name, skip by instance" idea from the header,
          in action. Ticking AutoFarm on a row adds the egg's NAME, so when the
          server later spawns a fresh egg of that type it gets farmed too. The
          per-instance processed table stops the loop re-targeting the exact
          object it just collected during the second or two before the server
          removes it.

    v2.4 CHANGED THREE THINGS HERE, ALL ABOUT CONSISTENCY:

          ORDER. The queue is walked nearest-first instead of alphabetically.
          Short flights are the ones that arrive; v2.3's alphabetical order sent
          the character back and forth across the map between pickups.

          PROOF. Each egg goes through collectEgg, which verifies the egg
          actually despawned and retries before giving up. v2.3 marked every egg
          processed the moment it had flown there, so a failed pickup was skipped
          permanently and looked, from the outside, like the farm "missing" eggs.

          PATIENCE. v2.3 used a "didWork" flag: if one full pass collected
          nothing it stopped, assuming deadlock. But a pass collects nothing
          whenever the queued types are simply mid-respawn, which is most of the
          time -- so AutoFarm kept quitting seconds after it started. v2.4 counts
          consecutive empty passes instead and only gives up after MaxIdlePasses
          (~12 seconds). Eggs that genuinely cannot be collected still leave the
          pool, because a failed egg is marked processed after its retries, so
          the loop can never spin forever on one target.

          Cleanup and the final status message now live in one `finish` helper.
          v2.3 wrote its last StatusLabel line after the while-loop, which is
          unreachable when the user presses Stop -- task.cancel kills the
          coroutine before it gets there.
============================================================================ ]]

-- Repaints one row's AutoFarm button to match whether its name is queued.
local function setAutoFarmButtonState(button, active)
    if not button then
        return
    end
    -- v2.6 signals state through the TEXT COLOUR, not the background. Every
    -- button now animates its own background on hover from a base colour it
    -- captured once, so writing the background here would be undone the next
    -- time the pointer left the button.
    button.Text       = active and "ON" or "FARM"
    button.TextColor3 = active
        and Color3.fromRGB(56, 214, 130)
        or  Color3.fromRGB(237, 240, 246)
end

-- (isValidEgg now lives up in Section 7, next to the other target helpers.)

-- Every spawned egg that is queued by name and not yet collected.
local function getAutoFarmEggs()
    local found = {}
    if not RenderedEggsFolder then
        return found
    end

    for _, egg in ipairs(RenderedEggsFolder:GetChildren()) do
        if isValidEgg(egg) and autoFarmEggs[egg.Name] and not autoFarmProcessed[egg] then
            -- Distance is measured ONCE, here, and stored. Calling
            -- getDistanceToTarget from inside the comparator would re-measure a
            -- moving target mid-sort; Lua detects the resulting inconsistency
            -- and throws "invalid order function for sorting", which inside a
            -- task.spawn kills the farm with no message at all.
            table.insert(found, { egg = egg, distance = getDistanceToTarget(egg) })
        end
    end

    -- v2.4: NEAREST FIRST. v2.3 sorted alphabetically, so the character bounced
    -- from one end of the map to the other between pickups. Sorting by distance
    -- collects exactly the same set with a fraction of the travel, and short
    -- flights are the ones that succeed.
    table.sort(found, function(a, b)
        if a.distance == b.distance then
            return a.egg.Name:lower() < b.egg.Name:lower()
        end
        return a.distance < b.distance
    end)

    local eggs = {}
    for index, entry in ipairs(found) do
        eggs[index] = entry.egg
    end
    return eggs
end

function stopAutoFarm()
    autoFarmActive = false
    stopMovement()                  -- restore collisions BEFORE cancelling
    autoFarmCurrentName = nil
    if autoFarmThread then
        task.cancel(autoFarmThread)
        autoFarmThread = nil
    end
    -- Critical: a cancelled coroutine never runs its own key-release, so if we
    -- were cancelled mid-holdEKey the E key would stay logically held down.
    releaseEKey()
end

-- Passed to collectEgg; see the note on isAutoBestEggActive.
local function isAutoFarmActive()
    return autoFarmActive
end

local function startAutoFarm()
    stopAutoFarm()
    stopAutoBestEgg()                        -- v2.5: modes are exclusive
    if stopAutoRare then stopAutoRare() end
    autoFarmActive = true

    if StopAutoFarmBtn then
        StopAutoFarmBtn.Visible = true
    end

    -- One exit path for every way the loop can end. v2.3 duplicated this cleanup
    -- in three places and its final status message was unreachable whenever the
    -- user pressed Stop, because task.cancel killed the coroutine first.
    local function finish(message)
        autoFarmActive      = false
        autoFarmCurrentName = nil
        autoFarmThread      = nil
        if StopAutoFarmBtn then
            StopAutoFarmBtn.Visible = false
        end
        releaseEKey()
        setStatus(message, Color3.fromRGB(180, 180, 180))
    end

    autoFarmThread = task.spawn(function()
        local idlePasses = 0

        while autoFarmActive do
            local eggs = getAutoFarmEggs()

            if #eggs == 0 then
                -- Nothing queued at all means the user genuinely has nothing
                -- selected, so stop immediately.
                if next(autoFarmEggs) == nil then
                    finish("● AutoFarm finished: nothing selected")
                    return
                end

                -- Otherwise the queued types are simply despawned right now.
                -- v2.3 treated this as "we are stuck" and quit on the very first
                -- empty pass, which is the reason AutoFarm so often stopped a
                -- few seconds after starting. Wait for the respawn instead, and
                -- only give up after MaxIdlePasses of nothing.
                idlePasses += 1
                if idlePasses >= Config.MaxIdlePasses then
                    finish("● AutoFarm finished: no selected eggs left")
                    return
                end

                setStatus("● AutoFarm: waiting for eggs to respawn",
                    Color3.fromRGB(220, 200, 120))
                task.wait(Config.IdlePassDelay)
            else
                idlePasses = 0

                -- Take the NEAREST egg only, then loop and re-measure. v2.3
                -- iterated a whole snapshot of the queue, but every pickup ends
                -- with a trip home, so by the second egg in that snapshot the
                -- ordering it was sorted into no longer describes where we are.
                -- Re-picking each time keeps every single flight a short one.
                local egg = eggs[1]

                if isValidEgg(egg) and not autoFarmProcessed[egg] then
                    local eggName = egg.Name
                    autoFarmCurrentName = eggName
                    setStatus("● AutoFarm: " .. eggName)

                    local collected = collectEgg(egg, Config.AutoFarmHoldTime, isAutoFarmActive)

                    if autoFarmActive then
                        teleportToHomePlot()  -- go home to hatch/store
                    end

                    -- Marked either way so the queue always advances, but an
                    -- unconfirmed pickup is now REPORTED rather than hidden.
                    -- collectEgg has already retried it InteractRetries times by
                    -- this point, so an egg that keeps failing leaves the pool
                    -- instead of stalling the farm on one unreachable target.
                    autoFarmProcessed[egg] = true
                    if not collected and autoFarmActive then
                        setStatus("● AutoFarm: " .. eggName .. " not confirmed",
                            Color3.fromRGB(230, 170, 60))
                    end
                end

                autoFarmCurrentName = nil
                task.wait(0.15)
            end
        end
    end)
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 16B  --  AUTOMATION, PART 5: THE RARE EGG HUNTER   (new in v2.5)
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Waits for a rare egg to spawn, flies to it, collects it, then goes home
          SLOWLY ENOUGH NOT TO BE OBVIOUS.

          while running:
              nearest un-taken egg matching Config.RareEggNames?
                  no  -> look again in RareScanDelay seconds
                  yes -> start the clock
                         fly to it (noclip) -> pin -> interact -> verify
                         fly to a point PlotHoldDistance studs SHORT of the plot
                         hold there until the clock reads SafeCycleMin
                         fly the last stretch onto the plot

    WHY THE CLOCK. Everything else in this file optimises for speed. This one
    deliberately does not. The detectable thing about a rare-egg bot is not any
    single action, it is the round trip: leaving your plot, crossing the map,
    picking up the rarest item in the game and being back home ten seconds later
    is not a shape a human produces. So the cycle is timed from the moment the
    hunter leaves, and any cycle that would finish early is padded out to
    SafeCycleMin before the last step is taken.

          The padding is spent OUTSIDE the plot on purpose. Standing near your
          own base for a few seconds is the most ordinary thing in the game;
          appearing on it the instant a rare egg vanishes from the map is not.

          SafeCycleMax is not enforced, only reported. Once a cycle has run long
          there is nothing useful left to do about it -- the information is for
          you, so you can tell a slow server apart from a broken script.

    WHY IT NEVER TELEPORTS. Every moveToModel call here passes forceFly = true,
    so the AutoFarm / Teleport switch is ignored for this button in both
    directions. Flying is a stream of plausible positions; a teleport is a single
    impossible one. A mode whose entire purpose is patience has no use for it.

    LEARN The flight marker trick. moveToModel takes an Instance, but "45 studs
          short of the plot" is a bare Vector3 with no object at it. Rather than
          duplicate the flight loop for raw coordinates, makeFlightMarker creates
          a transparent anchored Part there, flies to it, and destroys it. Parts
          created by an exploit on the client are never replicated to the server,
          so nobody else ever sees it.
============================================================================ ]]
local function isRareEgg(egg)
    local name = egg.Name:lower()
    for _, needle in ipairs(Config.RareEggNames) do
        -- Plain find (4th arg true), so a name like "Black-Hole (Limited)"
        -- cannot be read as Lua pattern syntax.
        if string.find(name, needle:lower(), 1, true) then
            return true
        end
    end
    return false
end

local function findNearestRareEgg()
    if not RenderedEggsFolder then
        return nil
    end

    -- autoFarmProcessed is shared with the queue farm on purpose: in both loops
    -- it means "this exact instance has been dealt with, do not target it again".
    local best         = nil
    local bestDistance = math.huge

    for _, egg in ipairs(RenderedEggsFolder:GetChildren()) do
        if isValidEgg(egg) and not autoFarmProcessed[egg] and isRareEgg(egg) then
            local distance = getDistanceToTarget(egg)
            if distance < bestDistance then
                best         = egg
                bestDistance = distance
            end
        end
    end

    return best
end

-- (makeFlightMarker moved up to Section 11 in v2.7; going home needs it too.)

-- Flies to a point short of the plot and returns the CFrame to hold there.
-- Returns nil if the flight did not arrive, in which case the caller simply
-- waits out the window wherever it happens to be.
local function moveNearPlot(plot)
    -- v2.7: measured from the LANDING point, not the plot's pivot. The pivot is
    -- underground (see Section 14), so the hold spot used to be computed around
    -- a point below the floor and inherited the same problem.
    local plotCFrame = getPlotLandingCFrame(plot)
    local root       = getRootPart()
    if not plotCFrame or not root then
        return nil
    end

    -- Stop short ALONG THE LINE WE ARE ALREADY ON: take the direction from the
    -- plot back towards wherever we are now. That reads as slowing down on the
    -- way in. Picking a fixed compass direction instead would make the hold spot
    -- jump to the far side of the plot on half the trips, which is a far odder
    -- path than the one it is trying to avoid.
    local away = root.Position - plotCFrame.Position
    away = Vector3.new(away.X, 0, away.Z)  -- flatten: hold beside it, not above

    if away.Magnitude < 1 then
        local look = plotCFrame.LookVector   -- already standing on the plot
        away = Vector3.new(look.X, 0, look.Z)
    end
    if away.Magnitude < 1 then
        away = Vector3.new(1, 0, 0)          -- degenerate; any direction will do
    end

    local holdPosition = plotCFrame.Position
        + (away.Unit * Config.PlotHoldDistance)
        + Vector3.new(0, Config.PlotHoldHeight, 0)

    local marker  = makeFlightMarker(holdPosition)
    local arrived = moveToModel(marker, true, Config.RareApproachSpeed)
    marker:Destroy()

    if not arrived then
        return nil
    end

    local currentRoot = getRootPart()
    return currentRoot and currentRoot.CFrame or CFrame.new(holdPosition)
end

local function waitOutSafeWindow(cycleStart, holdCFrame, isActive)
    -- The pin can legitimately need to run for the whole SafeCycleMin, which is
    -- far longer than the default PinMaxTime built for a 3 second interaction.
    local unpin = holdCFrame and pinRoot(holdCFrame, Config.SafeCycleMin + 5) or nil

    while isActive() do
        local elapsed = os.clock() - cycleStart
        if elapsed >= Config.SafeCycleMin then
            break
        end
        setStatus(string.format("● Rare: holding outside, %ds / %ds",
            math.floor(elapsed), Config.SafeCycleMin), Color3.fromRGB(230, 200, 90))
        task.wait(0.25)
    end

    if unpin then unpin() end
end

function stopAutoRare()
    autoRareActive = false
    setAutoRareButton(false)
    stopMovement()  -- restore collisions and release any pin BEFORE cancelling
    if autoRareThread then
        task.cancel(autoRareThread)
        autoRareThread = nil
    end
    releaseEKey()
end

local function isAutoRareActive()
    return autoRareActive
end

local function startAutoRare()
    stopAutoRare()
    stopAutoBestEgg()  -- the three automation modes are mutually exclusive
    stopAutoFarm()

    autoRareActive = true
    setAutoRareButton(true)

    autoRareThread = task.spawn(function()
        while autoRareActive do
            local egg = findNearestRareEgg()

            if not egg then
                setStatus("● Rare: watching for a spawn", Color3.fromRGB(200, 200, 140))
                task.wait(Config.RareScanDelay)
            else
                local eggName    = egg.Name
                local cycleStart = os.clock()  -- the clock starts when we LEAVE

                setStatus("● Rare: going for " .. eggName, Color3.fromRGB(255, 200, 60))

                -- v2.6: take the screen. The colours are chosen per egg type in
                -- Config.RareEggFX -- black and purple for a Blackhole, white
                -- and yellow for a Cherub -- so the alert tells you WHAT was
                -- found without you having to read anything.
                if ScreenFX.found then
                    ScreenFX.found(eggName)
                end

                -- forceFly = true and the slow speed on every move this loop
                -- makes. See Config.RareApproachSpeed for why slow is the point.
                local collected = collectEgg(egg, Config.RareEggHoldTime,
                    isAutoRareActive, true, Config.RareApproachSpeed)

                -- Marked either way. collectEgg has already retried it
                -- InteractRetries times, so an egg that still refuses to be
                -- collected is one the hunter should stop circling back to.
                autoFarmProcessed[egg] = true

                if not autoRareActive then break end

                if not collected then
                    setStatus("● Rare: " .. eggName .. " not confirmed",
                        Color3.fromRGB(230, 170, 60))
                end

                local plot = getHomePlot()
                if not plot then
                    setStatus("● Rare: your plot was not found", Color3.fromRGB(255, 100, 100))
                    task.wait(1)
                else
                    local holdCFrame = moveNearPlot(plot)

                    if autoRareActive then
                        waitOutSafeWindow(cycleStart, holdCFrame, isAutoRareActive)
                    end

                    if autoRareActive then
                        -- The last stretch, and the one relocation this cycle is
                        -- allowed: force = true, because it comes after the full
                        -- safety window rather than on top of another trip.
                        teleportToHomePlot(true, Config.RareApproachSpeed, true)

                        local elapsed = os.clock() - cycleStart
                        if elapsed > Config.SafeCycleMax then
                            setStatus(string.format("● Rare: %s done -- cycle ran long (%ds)",
                                eggName, math.floor(elapsed)), Color3.fromRGB(230, 170, 60))
                        else
                            setStatus(string.format("● Rare: %s done in %ds",
                                eggName, math.floor(elapsed)))
                        end
                    end
                end

                task.wait(0.3)
            end
        end
    end)
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 16C  --  ANTI-AFK   (new in v2.8)
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Keeps Roblox from disconnecting you for inactivity.

    WHY   Writing HumanoidRootPart.CFrame moves your character but is not INPUT.
          Roblox's idle timer only watches the mouse and keyboard, so a script
          that farms all night still looks, to the timer, like an empty chair.

    THE RIGHT CLICK is the important idea here. VirtualUser:ClickButton2 resets
    the idle timer while doing nothing else at all: it does not move you, Roblox
    games almost never bind right-click to anything, and it cannot land on a GUI
    button by accident. That makes it the cheapest possible lie to tell the idle
    timer, and it is what every serious anti-AFK does.

    VirtualUser is worth a note: it is the service Roblox uses to replay recorded
    input, and calling it from a normal game script is blocked. An executor
    environment allows it -- and unlike VirtualInputManager, input it produces
    counts as activity for the idle timer. That difference is the whole reason
    this section uses a different service from holdEKey.

    THE JUMP GATE. A jump is a real physical action, so it has to stay out of the
    way of the ones the script is already performing:

        movementActive  -> we are mid-flight; a jump fights the movement loop
                           for control of the character
        pinOwner ~= 0   -> we are pinned on an egg or waiting out the safety
                           window; a jump breaks the pin and the pickup fails,
                           which is precisely the bug v2.4 existed to fix
        already airborne-> stacking jump requests does nothing useful

    Whatever is left is a character standing still with nothing happening, which
    is both safe to interrupt and the moment a jump looks most like a real player
    with nothing to do.
============================================================================ ]]
local function antiAfkClick()
    -- pcall: VirtualUser is not exposed by every executor, and a missing service
    -- must not take down the loop that calls this.
    pcall(function()
        VirtualUser:CaptureController()
        VirtualUser:ClickButton2(Vector2.new())
    end)
end

local function antiAfkHop()
    if not Config.AntiAFKJump then return false end
    if movementActive or pinOwner ~= 0 then return false end  -- script is busy

    local character = getCharacter()
    local humanoid  = character and character:FindFirstChildOfClass("Humanoid")
    if not humanoid then return false end

    local state = humanoid:GetState()
    if state == Enum.HumanoidStateType.Freefall
        or state == Enum.HumanoidStateType.Jumping
        or state == Enum.HumanoidStateType.Dead then
        return false
    end

    humanoid:ChangeState(Enum.HumanoidStateType.Jump)
    return true
end

local function antiAfkPulse()
    if not antiAfkEnabled then return end
    antiAfkClick()
    antiAfkHop()
end

--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 17  --  THE INTERFACE, REBUILT IN v2.6
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHY THIS WAS REWRITTEN

    Every version up to v2.5 positioned each widget with a hard-coded pixel
    offset, twice -- once in setPCMode and once in setMobileMode:

        AutoBestEggBtn.Position = UDim2.new(0, 0, 0, 92)
        TPHomeBtn.Position      = UDim2.new(0, 0, 0, 129)
        ...

    That has three consequences, and you hit all three:

      1. THE LIST FELL OUT OF THE WINDOW. The offsets were tuned against one
         window height. Nothing checked that the last element still fitted, so
         the egg list simply ran past the bottom edge.
      2. ADDING ONE BUTTON MEANT RE-TYPING EVERY NUMBER BELOW IT, in both
         layouts, plus the window height. v2.5's Rare Egg Hunter needed eleven
         edits to add one row.
      3. THE TWO LAYOUTS DRIFTED APART, because nothing forced them to agree.

    v2.6 deletes all of it. The rules now are:

      * NOTHING IS POSITIONED BY HAND. Every stack is a UIListLayout, so an
        element's position is derived from the ones above it. Insert a row
        anywhere and everything below moves by itself.
      * THE BODY IS A SCROLLING FRAME with AutomaticCanvasSize. Content that
        does not fit becomes scrollable instead of becoming invisible. It is
        now structurally impossible for a control to end up outside the window.
      * THE WINDOW IS CLAMPED TO THE VIEWPORT at build time, so it cannot open
        off-screen on a small phone.
      * ONE LAYOUT, TWO SIZES. setPCMode / setMobileMode are gone; applyDevice
        sets a size and a UIScale and that is the entire difference.
      * COLOURS AND SPACING COME FROM ONE TABLE (Theme, below). Changing the
        accent colour is one edit, not forty.

    LEARN The general lesson is worth more than the script: absolute positioning
          is a layout that only works for the exact content you had when you
          wrote it. Layout objects express the RELATIONSHIP between elements, so
          they keep working when the content changes.
============================================================================ ]]

--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    PRIMITIVE HELPERS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Tiny wrappers around the Roblox UI modifier objects. They exist so the
    component builders below read as a description of the interface rather than
    as a wall of Instance.new calls.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function addCorner(instance, radius)
    local corner = Instance.new("UICorner")
    corner.CornerRadius = UDim.new(0, radius or Theme.Radius)
    corner.Parent       = instance
    return corner
end

local function addStroke(instance, color, transparency, thickness)
    local stroke = Instance.new("UIStroke")
    stroke.Color        = color or Theme.Line
    stroke.Transparency = transparency or 0.45
    stroke.Thickness    = thickness or 1
    stroke.Parent       = instance
    return stroke
end

local function addPadding(instance, all, horizontal)
    local pad = Instance.new("UIPadding")
    local v   = UDim.new(0, all)
    local h   = UDim.new(0, horizontal or all)
    pad.PaddingTop    = v
    pad.PaddingBottom = v
    pad.PaddingLeft   = h
    pad.PaddingRight  = h
    pad.Parent        = instance
    return pad
end

-- A vertical stack. Children are ordered by LayoutOrder and spaced by `gap`.
local function addVStack(instance, gap)
    local layout = Instance.new("UIListLayout")
    layout.FillDirection       = Enum.FillDirection.Vertical
    layout.SortOrder           = Enum.SortOrder.LayoutOrder
    layout.Padding             = UDim.new(0, gap or Theme.Gap)
    layout.HorizontalAlignment = Enum.HorizontalAlignment.Left
    layout.Parent              = instance
    return layout
end

local function addHStack(instance, gap, alignRight)
    local layout = Instance.new("UIListLayout")
    layout.FillDirection       = Enum.FillDirection.Horizontal
    layout.SortOrder           = Enum.SortOrder.LayoutOrder
    layout.Padding             = UDim.new(0, gap or 6)
    layout.VerticalAlignment   = Enum.VerticalAlignment.Center
    layout.HorizontalAlignment = alignRight
        and Enum.HorizontalAlignment.Right
        or  Enum.HorizontalAlignment.Left
    layout.Parent              = instance
    return layout
end

local function makeLabel(parent, text, size, color, order)
    local label = Instance.new("TextLabel")
    label.BackgroundTransparency = 1
    label.Text                   = text
    label.TextSize               = size or Theme.BodySize
    label.TextColor3             = color or Theme.Text
    label.Font                   = Enum.Font.GothamMedium
    label.TextXAlignment         = Enum.TextXAlignment.Left
    label.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, size and (size + 6) or 19)
    label.LayoutOrder            = order or 1
    label.Parent                 = parent
    return label
end


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    INTERACTION FEEDBACK
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Every clickable surface gets the same hover / press behaviour, driven from a
    base colour captured once. v2.3's version read the widget's live colour on
    every hover and nudged it, so an interrupted tween fed its own half-finished
    value back in and buttons drifted permanently lighter.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function makeInteractive(button, baseColor, hoverColor, pressColor)
    local base  = baseColor  or Theme.Control
    local hover = hoverColor or Theme.Line
    local press = pressColor or Theme.Raised

    button.AutoButtonColor  = false
    button.BackgroundColor3 = base

    button.MouseEnter:Connect(function()
        tween(button, { BackgroundColor3 = hover }, 0.12)
    end)
    button.MouseLeave:Connect(function()
        tween(button, { BackgroundColor3 = base }, 0.14)
    end)
    button.MouseButton1Down:Connect(function()
        tween(button, { BackgroundColor3 = press }, 0.06)
    end)
    button.MouseButton1Up:Connect(function()
        tween(button, { BackgroundColor3 = hover }, 0.10)
    end)
end

--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    COMPONENTS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Each builder returns the object a caller needs to keep, and hides the rest.
    This is the part that makes adding a feature cheap: a new control is one call
    to makeToggle, with no positions to work out and nothing below it to move.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]

-- A titled panel. Returns the frame you put content into.
local function makeCard(parent, titleText, order)
    local card = Instance.new("Frame")
    card.Name             = "Card_" .. titleText:gsub("%s", "")
    card.BackgroundColor3 = Theme.Surface
    card.BorderSizePixel  = 0
    card.Size             = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, 0)
    card.AutomaticSize    = Enum.AutomaticSize.Y  -- grows to fit its children
    card.LayoutOrder      = order or 1
    card.Parent           = parent
    addCorner(card)
    addStroke(card, Theme.Line, 0.55)
    addPadding(card, Theme.Pad, Theme.Pad)

    local stack = Instance.new("Frame")
    stack.Name                   = "Stack"
    stack.BackgroundTransparency = 1
    stack.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, 0)
    stack.AutomaticSize          = Enum.AutomaticSize.Y
    stack.Parent                 = card
    addVStack(stack, Theme.Gap)

    local heading = makeLabel(stack, titleText:upper(), Theme.TinySize, Theme.TextFaint, 0)
    heading.Font = Enum.Font.GothamBold

    return stack, card
end

-- A full-width action button.

    local button = Instance.new("TextButton")

--[[ A switch. Returns a handle with set(active) / get(), NOT the raw button,
     because these can now be flipped from three different places -- the user
     clicking, another automation mode starting, or a loop finishing on its own.
     Routing every repaint through one function is what keeps the label honest.
]]
local function makeToggle(parent, labelText, hintText, order, activeColor)
    local accent = activeColor or Theme.Good

    local row = Instance.new("TextButton")
    row.Name             = "Toggle_" .. labelText:gsub("%W", "")
    row.Size             = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, hintText and 42 or Theme.RowHeight)
    row.BackgroundColor3  = Theme.Raised
    row.BorderSizePixel  = 0
    row.Text             = ""
    row.AutoButtonColor  = false
    row.LayoutOrder      = order or 1
    row.Parent           = parent
    addCorner(row, Theme.RadiusSm)
    addPadding(row, 8, 10)

    local title = Instance.new("TextLabel")
    title.BackgroundTransparency = 1
    title.Text                   = labelText
    title.TextSize               = Theme.BodySize
    title.TextColor3             = Theme.Text
    title.Font                   = Enum.Font.GothamMedium
    title.TextXAlignment         = Enum.TextXAlignment.Left
    title.Position               = UDim2.new(0, 0, 0, 0)
    title.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, -54, 0, hintText and 16 or 20)
    title.Parent                 = row

    if hintText then
        local hint = Instance.new("TextLabel")
        hint.BackgroundTransparency = 1
        hint.Text                   = hintText
        hint.TextSize               = Theme.TinySize
        hint.TextColor3             = Theme.TextFaint
        hint.Font                   = Enum.Font.Gotham
        hint.TextXAlignment         = Enum.TextXAlignment.Left
        hint.TextTruncate           = Enum.TextTruncate.AtEnd
        hint.Position               = UDim2.new(0, 0, 0, 17)
        hint.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, -54, 0, 14)
        hint.Parent                 = row
    end

    -- The switch itself: a track with a knob that slides.
    local track = Instance.new("Frame")
    track.AnchorPoint      = Vector2.new(1, 0.5)
    track.Position         = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0.5, 0)
    track.Size             = UDim2.new(0, 40, 0, 22)
    track.BackgroundColor3 = Theme.Control
    track.BorderSizePixel  = 0
    track.Parent           = row
    addCorner(track, 11)

    local knob = Instance.new("Frame")
    knob.AnchorPoint      = Vector2.new(0, 0.5)
    knob.Position         = UDim2.new(0, 3, 0.5, 0)
    knob.Size             = UDim2.new(0, 16, 0, 16)
    knob.BackgroundColor3 = Theme.TextDim
    knob.BorderSizePixel  = 0
    -- Not zero on purpose. The glass pass only touches elements sitting at
    -- exactly 0, so a widget that must stay readable opts out simply by not
    -- being at 0. One rule, no exception list to keep in sync.
    knob.BackgroundTransparency = 0.05
    knob.Parent           = track
    addCorner(knob, 8)

    local state  = false
    local handle = {}

    function handle.set(active)
        state = active and true or false
        tween(track, { BackgroundColor3 = state and accent or Theme.Control }, 0.15)
        tween(knob, {
            Position        = state and UDim2.new(1, -19, 0.5, 0) or UDim2.new(0, 3, 0.5, 0),
            BackgroundColor3 = state and Color3.fromRGB(255, 255, 255) or Theme.TextDim,
        }, 0.15)
        title.TextColor3 = state and accent or Theme.Text
    end

    function handle.get()
        return state
    end

    handle.Row = row
    row.MouseEnter:Connect(function() tween(row, { BackgroundColor3 = Theme.Control }, 0.12) end)
    row.MouseLeave:Connect(function() tween(row, { BackgroundColor3 = Theme.Raised }, 0.14) end)

    handle.set(false)
    return handle
end

-- Two mutually exclusive buttons sharing one pill. Returns both buttons, because

local function makeSegmented(parent, leftText, rightText, order)
    local holder = Instance.new("Frame")

    holder.Size             = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, 34)
    holder.LayoutOrder      = order or 1
    holder.Parent           = parent
    addCorner(holder, Theme.RadiusSm)
    addPadding(holder, 3, 3)

    local function segment(text, index)
        local button = Instance.new("TextButton")

        button.Position         = index == 1 and UDim2.new(0, 0, 0, 0) or UDim2.new(0.5, 2, 0, 0)
        button.BackgroundColor3 = Theme.Raised
        button.BorderSizePixel  = 0
        button.Text             = text
        button.TextSize         = Theme.SmallSize
        button.TextColor3       = Theme.TextDim
        button.Font             = Enum.Font.GothamMedium
        button.AutoButtonColor  = false
        button.Parent           = holder
        addCorner(button, 5)
        return button
    end

    return segment(leftText, 1), segment(rightText, 2)
end


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 18  --  SCREEN EFFECTS  (new in v2.6)
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    WHAT  Full-screen colour flashes: a short splash when the Rare Egg Hunter is
          armed, and a two-colour blink when it locks onto a rare egg.

    WHY   The hunter can sit idle for a long time waiting for a spawn. The whole
          point of an alert is that it works when you are NOT looking at a small
          status line in the corner of a menu -- so the alert takes the screen.

    THE COLOURS come from Config.RareEggFX, matched against the egg's name:
          Blackhole -> black and purple
          Cherub    -> white and yellow
          anything else -> Config.DefaultFXColors

    HOW IT IS BUILT
      * Its own ScreenGui with a high DisplayOrder, so it draws over the menu and
        over the game's own UI.
      * IgnoreGuiInset = true, so it covers the top bar area too.
      * The overlay Frame has Active = false and no interactive children. A
        non-Active Frame does not absorb clicks, so the effect never costs you a
        tap -- important, since it fires while you are playing.

    THE TOKEN GUARD is the same generation-counter trick used by pinRoot. Two
    rare eggs found back to back would otherwise leave two blink loops fighting
    over one frame's transparency, and the overlay could be left visible. Each
    run claims the next token and stops the moment the token changes.
============================================================================ ]]
local FXGui = Instance.new("ScreenGui")
FXGui.Name             = "RenderedEggsESP_FX"

local fxToken = 0

    fxToken += 1
    FXOverlay.BackgroundTransparency = 1

-- One quick pulse in a single colour.
local function fxSplash(color, peak, duration)
    if not screenFXEnabled then return end

    fxToken += 1
    local myToken = fxToken
    local half    = (duration or Config.SplashTime) / 2

    FXOverlay.BackgroundColor3       = color
    FXOverlay.BackgroundTransparency = 1

    task.spawn(function()
        tween(FXOverlay, { BackgroundTransparency = peak or Config.BlinkPeak }, half)
        task.wait(half)
        if fxToken ~= myToken then return end
        tween(FXOverlay, { BackgroundTransparency = 1 }, half)
        task.wait(half)
        if fxToken == myToken then
            FXOverlay.BackgroundTransparency = 1
        end
    end)
end

-- Alternate between two colours for `duration` seconds.
local function fxBlink(colorA, colorB, duration)
    if not screenFXEnabled then return end

    fxToken += 1
    local myToken = fxToken

    task.spawn(function()
        local deadline = os.clock() + (duration or Config.BlinkDuration)
        local flip     = false

        while os.clock() < deadline and fxToken == myToken do
            flip = not flip
            FXOverlay.BackgroundColor3 = flip and colorA or colorB
            -- Snap ON, ease OFF: an instant edge reads as an alarm, a symmetric
            -- fade reads as an animation and is much easier to miss.
            FXOverlay.BackgroundTransparency = Config.BlinkPeak
            tween(FXOverlay, { BackgroundTransparency = 0.85 }, Config.BlinkInterval)
            task.wait(Config.BlinkInterval)
        end

        if fxToken == myToken then
            tween(FXOverlay, { BackgroundTransparency = 1 }, 0.25)
            task.wait(0.25)
            if fxToken == myToken then
                FXOverlay.BackgroundTransparency = 1
            end
        end
    end)
end

-- Look up the colour pair for an egg name.
local function fxColorsFor(eggName)
    local lowered = eggName:lower()
    for _, entry in ipairs(Config.RareEggFX) do
        if string.find(lowered, entry.match:lower(), 1, true) then
            return entry.a, entry.b
        end
    end
    return Config.DefaultFXColors.a, Config.DefaultFXColors.b
end

--[[ The safety net behind the anchor fix. Anything that can move or resize the
     window calls this afterwards, and it pulls the frame back inside the
     viewport. Even if a future change, a screen rotation or a resolution switch
     leaves the menu half off the edge, the next drag or resize recovers it --
     you can never end up unable to reach the title bar.

     AbsoluteSize is read rather than MainFrame.Size because UIScale means the
     two are not the same number. ]]
local function clampWindow()
    local camera   = Workspace.CurrentCamera
    local viewport = camera and camera.ViewportSize or Vector2.new(1280, 720)
    local size     = MainFrame.AbsoluteSize

    local maxX = math.max(0, viewport.X - size.X)
    local maxY = math.max(0, viewport.Y - size.Y)

    MainFrame.Position = UDim2.fromOffset(
        math.clamp(MainFrame.Position.X.Offset, 0, maxX),
        math.clamp(MainFrame.Position.Y.Offset, 0, maxY)
    )
end

--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    THE GLASS PASS   (v2.7)
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    One walk of the finished tree turns every solid surface semi-transparent, so
    the game stays visible behind the menu.

    Doing it in a pass rather than in each builder means a control added later is
    covered automatically, with no way to forget. The rule is deliberately dumb:
    "if this element is at exactly 0, move it to Theme.Alpha". Anything that must
    stay solid opts out by being created at some other value -- see the switch
    knob above.

    Text gets a faint stroke at the same time. Light text at 13px over a bright,
    moving game is genuinely hard to read once the panel behind it is half gone;
    the outline costs nothing and makes the menu legible over any scene.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local function applyGlass(root)
    for _, element in ipairs(root:GetDescendants()) do
        if element:IsA("GuiObject") then
            if element.BackgroundTransparency == 0 then
                element.BackgroundTransparency = Theme.Alpha
            end
            if (element:IsA("TextLabel") or element:IsA("TextButton") or element:IsA("TextBox"))
                and element.TextStrokeTransparency == 1 then
                element.TextStrokeTransparency = 0.75
            end
        end
    end
end

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

    end)

--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 21  --  POPULATING THE LIST
============================================================================ ]]
local function getEggsForList()
    local entries = {}
    if not RenderedEggsFolder then
        return entries
    end

    for _, egg in ipairs(RenderedEggsFolder:GetChildren()) do
        if egg:IsA("Model") or egg:IsA("BasePart") then
            -- Measured once, up front. Measuring inside the comparator lets the
            -- value change mid-sort, and Lua raises "invalid order function for
            -- sorting" when it does -- thrown out of populateList.
            table.insert(entries, { egg = egg, distance = getDistanceToTarget(egg) })
        end
    end

    table.sort(entries, function(a, b)
        if sortMode == "Distance" then
            if a.distance == b.distance then
                return a.egg.Name:lower() < b.egg.Name:lower()
            end
            return a.distance < b.distance
        end
        return a.egg.Name:lower() < b.egg.Name:lower()
    end)

    local eggs = {}
    for index, entry in ipairs(entries) do
        eggs[index] = entry.egg
    end
    return eggs
end

local function clearEggList()
    for _, child in ipairs(ScrollList:GetChildren()) do
        if child ~= UIListLayout then
            child:Destroy()
        end
    end
    table.clear(listDistanceLabels)
end

local function populateList()
    clearEggList()

    if not RenderedEggsFolder then
        EggCountLabel.Text = "0 eggs  ·  0 shown"
        return
    end

    local query        = currentSearchQuery:lower()
    local eggs         = getEggsForList()
    local visibleCount = 0
    local itemHeight   = isMobileMode and 32 or 34
    local textSize     = isMobileMode and 11 or 12

    for _, egg in ipairs(eggs) do
        -- Plain find (4th arg true): a "-" or "(" in an egg name cannot be read
        -- as Lua pattern syntax and break the search.
        local matches = query == "" or string.find(egg.Name:lower(), query, 1, true)
        if matches then
            visibleCount += 1
            createEggListItem(egg, itemHeight, textSize)
        end
    end

    EggCountLabel.Text = string.format("%d eggs  ·  %d shown", #eggs, visibleCount)

    if visibleCount == 0 then
        local empty = Instance.new("TextLabel")
        empty.Name                   = "NoResultsLabel"
        empty.Size                   = UDim2.new(1, -6, 0, 34)
        empty.BackgroundTransparency = 1
        empty.Text                   = query == "" and "No eggs in the world right now"
                                       or ("Nothing matching \"" .. currentSearchQuery .. "\"")
        empty.TextColor3             = Theme.TextFaint
        empty.TextSize               = Theme.SmallSize
        empty.Font                   = Enum.Font.Gotham
        empty.Parent                 = ScrollList
    end
end

-- Debounced rebuild. The spawn/despawn events fire in bursts on a busy server,
-- and a full teardown per event costs frames -- frames the flight loop needs,
-- because it steps by delta time and takes huge imprecise jumps when the frame
-- rate collapses.
local listRebuildQueued = false

local function requestListRebuild()
    if listRebuildQueued then return end
    listRebuildQueued = true
    task.delay(Config.ListRebuildDebounce, function()
        listRebuildQueued = false
        if ListContainerFrame.Visible then
            populateList()
        end
    end)
end

--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    DRAGGING
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    The classic three-signal pattern. The movement is applied from the GLOBAL
    UserInputService signal, not the title bar's own, because a fast drag moves
    the pointer off the bar and the bar would stop receiving events mid-drag.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
local dragging      = false
local dragInput     = nil
local dragStart     = nil
local startPosition = nil

TopBar.InputBegan:Connect(function(input)
    if input.UserInputType == Enum.UserInputType.MouseButton1
        or input.UserInputType == Enum.UserInputType.Touch then
        dragging      = true
        dragStart     = input.Position
        startPosition = MainFrame.Position

        input.Changed:Connect(function()
            if input.UserInputState == Enum.UserInputState.End then
                dragging = false
                clampWindow()  -- released off the edge? pull it back in
            end
        end)
    end
end)

TopBar.InputChanged:Connect(function(input)
    if input.UserInputType == Enum.UserInputType.MouseMovement
        or input.UserInputType == Enum.UserInputType.Touch then
        dragInput = input
    end
end)

UserInputService.InputChanged:Connect(function(input)
    if input == dragInput and dragging then
        local delta = input.Position - dragStart
        MainFrame.Position = UDim2.new(
            startPosition.X.Scale,
            startPosition.X.Offset + delta.X,
            startPosition.Y.Scale,
            startPosition.Y.Offset + delta.Y
        )
    end
end)


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 23  --  KEYBOARD
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Two things worth remembering here:

      1. The rebind branch comes FIRST and returns immediately, so while you are
         rebinding the key is swallowed and stored rather than acted on.
      2. gameProcessed is true when Roblox already consumed the key -- above all
         when you are typing in a TextBox or the chat. Without that check,
         typing "t" into the search box would teleport you home. Forgetting
         gameProcessed is the most common bug in Roblox hotkey code.
============================================================================ ]]
UserInputService.InputBegan:Connect(function(input, gameProcessed)
    if listeningForKey then
        if input.UserInputType == Enum.UserInputType.Keyboard then
            tpKeybind             = input.KeyCode
            listeningForKey       = false
            KeybindBtn.Text       = "Hotkey: [" .. tpKeybind.Name .. "]"
            KeybindBtn.TextColor3 = Theme.TextDim
        end
        return
    end

    if gameProcessed then return end

    if input.UserInputType == Enum.UserInputType.Keyboard
        and input.KeyCode == tpKeybind then
        -- force = true, same reasoning as the TP Home button: you pressed it.
        teleportToHomePlot(nil, nil, true)
    end
end)


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 24  --  THE REFRESH LOOP
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Runs at 5 Hz, not per frame. Distances only need to LOOK live; recomputing
    them sixty times a second costs twelve times more for no visible benefit.

    "while ScreenGui.Parent" makes the loop die with the menu, so re-executing
    the script does not leave old loops running forever.
============================================================================ ]]
task.spawn(function()
    while ScreenGui.Parent do
        for egg, data in pairs(eggData) do
            if egg.Parent and data.NameBillboard and data.NameBillboard.Enabled then
                updateEggLabel(egg)
            end
        end

        for egg, label in pairs(listDistanceLabels) do
            if egg.Parent and label.Parent then
                local distance = getDistanceToTarget(egg)
                label.Text = distance == math.huge
                    and "--"
                    or string.format("%dm", math.floor(distance + 0.5))
            else
                listDistanceLabels[egg] = nil
            end
        end

        task.wait(Config.ListRefreshRate)
    end
end)


--[[ ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ANTI-AFK, THE TWO TRIGGERS
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Idled is the accurate one -- Roblox tells you it has decided you are idle, so
    the response lands exactly when it is needed and at no other time. The timed
    loop is the backstop, because how faithfully Idled fires varies between
    executors, and a missed kick costs a whole night of farming.

    Both go through antiAfkPulse, which re-reads the live flag, so switching
    Anti-AFK off takes effect immediately rather than at the next tick.

    Re-running the whole script leaves the previous Idled connection alive. It is
    harmless -- the worst case is a second right click nobody can see -- and the
    timed loop dies with its menu, because it is bound to ScreenGui.Parent.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ]]
LocalPlayer.Idled:Connect(function()
    antiAfkPulse()
end)

task.spawn(function()
    while ScreenGui.Parent do
        task.wait(Config.AntiAFKInterval)
        antiAfkPulse()
    end
end)

--[[ Respawn recovery. Dying mid-flight used to poison the rest of the session:
     movementActive could be left true, and a true movementActive makes every
     later move return false immediately -- the farm keeps running, flies
     nowhere and collects nothing.

     The automation loops are deliberately NOT stopped here. Dying does not mean
     you wanted to stop farming, and every loop re-checks its flags and
     re-validates its target after each yield, so they pick up the new character
     on their own. ]]
LocalPlayer.CharacterAdded:Connect(function()
    movementActive     = false
    movementHumanoid   = nil
    movementAutoRotate = nil
    movementPartsState = nil  -- the old snapshot points at destroyed parts
    pinToken += 1             -- release any pin loop the old character was under
    cachedHomePlot     = nil
    releaseEKey()
end)


--[[ ============================================================================
    SECTION 26  --  DEVICE SIZING AND ENTRY POINT
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    This replaces v2.5's setPCMode and setMobileMode, which were 120 lines of
    duplicated pixel offsets between them. There is one layout now; the device
    choice only picks a size and a scale.

    The clamp against the camera's viewport is what guarantees the window opens
    fully on screen, whatever the device -- including the case the old code got
    wrong, where a phone shorter than MobileHeight simply lost the bottom of the
    menu off the edge of the display.
============================================================================ ]]
local function applyDevice(mobile)
    isMobileMode = mobile

    local camera   = Workspace.CurrentCamera
    local viewport = camera and camera.ViewportSize or Vector2.new(1280, 720)

    local width  = math.min(mobile and Config.MobileWidth  or Config.PCWidth,  viewport.X - 24)
    local height = math.min(mobile and Config.MobileHeight or Config.PCHeight, viewport.Y - 48)

    currentExpandedWidth  = width
    currentExpandedHeight = height

    MainFrame.Size = UDim2.fromOffset(width, height)
    -- Centred by arithmetic rather than by anchor point, because the anchor is
    -- now the top-left corner. Same visual result, and resizing no longer drags
    -- the window around with it.
    MainFrame.Position = UDim2.fromOffset(
        math.floor((viewport.X - width) / 2),
        math.floor((viewport.Y - height) / 2)
    )
    UIScaleObj.Scale = mobile and 0.94 or 1

    -- The list gets a fixed viewport of its own; the body scrolls if the cards
    -- above it do not leave room.
    ScrollList.Size = UDim2.new(1, 0, 0, mobile and 148 or 188)

    if DeviceFrame then
        DeviceFrame:Destroy()
        DeviceFrame = nil
    end

    MainFrame.Visible = true
    clampWindow()
    populateList()
    setStatus("● System ready")
end

PCBtn.MouseButton1Click:Connect(function()
    applyDevice(false)
end)

    applyDevice(true)
end)

--[[ ============================================================================

    APPENDIX A0  --  WHAT v2.6 CHANGED
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
     1. The egg list rendered outside the window. Cause: every widget was placed
        at a hard-coded pixel offset, and nothing verified the last one still
        fitted inside the frame. Fixed structurally, not by re-tuning numbers:
        layouts derive each position from the elements above it, cards size to
        their content, and the body scrolls when the content is taller than the
        window.
     2. setPCMode / setMobileMode were 120 lines of duplicated offsets that
        drifted apart. Replaced by applyDevice: one size, one UIScale.
     3. The window could open off the edge of a small screen. Now clamped to the
        camera's viewport.
     4. Adding one control used to mean editing eleven numbers across two layout
        functions plus the window height. It is now one makeToggle call.
     5. Toggle state lived in button text set by the click handler, so a mode
        switched off by another mode starting kept claiming it was ON. State now
        flows through switch handles that start/stop repaint.
     6. The per-row FARM button signalled state with its background colour, which
        the hover animation overwrote. It uses the text colour now.
     7. Added: full-screen alerts for the Rare Egg Hunter, colour-coded per egg
        type, with their own switch.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    APPENDIX A  --  WHAT v2.4 FIXED
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Every item here was a real defect in v2.3. The first five are the ones that
    made farming unreliable; the rest are correctness and cost.

    CONSISTENCY
     1. Flight momentum was never cleared, so switching collisions back on flung
        the character off the egg.                     -> zeroVelocity, Section 11
     2. Nothing held the character in place during the E hold, so gravity pulled
        it out of prompt range mid-interaction.        -> pinRoot, Section 11
     3. Eggs were marked "processed" without any check that the pickup worked, so
        a failure was skipped forever, never retried.  -> collectEgg, Section 15
     4. Interaction was E-key only, at the mercy of HoldDuration and dropped key
        events.                                        -> interactWith, Section 15
     5. AutoFarm quit on the first pass where every queued egg happened to be
        despawned, i.e. seconds after starting.        -> idlePasses, Section 16

    TARGETING
     6. The flight destination was snapshotted before take-off, so a target that
        moved or finished streaming in was flown past. -> re-read each frame
     7. AutoFarm collected in alphabetical order, criss-crossing the map; long
        flights are the ones that time out.            -> nearest-first
     8. Auto Best Egg took the first matching egg in the folder, not the closest.
     9. Arrival radius of 2 studs is smaller than one frame's step at 500 studs/s
        on a low frame rate, so the loop could orbit its target until timeout.
    10. A blocked path always burned the full timeout. -> StuckTimeout

    STATE AND LIFECYCLE
    11. Dying mid-flight left movementActive true, which silently disabled every
        later move for the rest of the session.        -> CharacterAdded reset
    12. Cancelling a farm during an interaction left the character pinned in
        place forever.                                 -> pinToken generation
    13. stopMovement forced AutoRotate back to true instead of restoring it.
    14. Teleport mode bypassed the one-move-at-a-time lock entirely.
    15. autoFarmProcessed was never cleared in removeEggData -- a slow leak.

    CORRECTNESS AND COST
    16. Both sort comparators measured distance mid-sort, which Lua rejects with
        "invalid order function for sorting" -- thrown inside task.spawn, that
        kills the loop with no message.                -> measure, then sort
    17. The list rebuilt fully on every egg spawn/despawn. -> debounced
    18. List row distances were frozen at build time, so "Sort: Distance" went
        stale as you moved.                            -> live refresh
    19. The ESP refresh loop was gated on mainESPActive, so green per-egg labels
        never updated their distance.
    20. Hover tweens read their own in-flight value and drifted permanently.
    21. The press animation tweened Size to its own value: dead code.
    22. The row TP button ignored the AutoFarm / Teleport mode switch.
    23. AutoFarm's final status message was unreachable when you pressed Stop.
    24. CoreGui was fetched and never used.

    STILL TRUE, BY DESIGN
      * Config.MovementSpeed = 500 is roughly 30x walk speed and, combined with
        noclip, remains the loudest anticheat signal in the file. Lower it if you
        care more about looking plausible than about throughput.
